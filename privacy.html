Privacy Policy

Calcora is designed to provide simple online calculators without requiring users to create an account.

Information We Collect

Calcora does not intentionally require personal information to use its calculators. Calculator inputs are processed in your browser unless a specific feature states otherwise.

Local Storage

Some features, such as Favorites, Recently Used calculators and display preferences, may use your browser's local storage. This information remains on your device and is used to improve your experience.

Cookies and Advertising

Calcora may use cookies or similar technologies in the future, including technologies provided by advertising or analytics services. Any such services will have their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Calcora develops new features or services.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Calcora team.
